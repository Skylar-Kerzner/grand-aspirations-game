# Dynamic career offers

## Goal
Turn promotions into choices with uncertain pay rather than a fixed ladder.

## Changes
- Keep the existing career levels and education requirements, but generate three competing offers whenever promotion requirements are met.
- Each offer gets a fitting alternate position title and employer, plus a salary rolled within a visible market range around that career level's base pay.
- Let the player accept one offer or reroll after gaining more experience, making luck matter without allowing endless free rerolls.
- Store the accepted title, employer, and salary so daily pay, extra shifts, events, review totals, and the workplace display all use the actual job.
- Show the current salary versus the market range, and keep future roles partially hidden until reachable.
- Preserve old saves by treating their existing fixed job as the midpoint salary for that level.

## Verification
- Check promotion eligibility, offer generation, accepted salary calculations, save migration, top-career compensation, and mobile presentation.
